More joy in aquarium ownership

More time enjoying your aquarium.
Less time maintaining it.

The fish. The beauty. A little world you love watching grow. That’s what brings an aquarium into your life. Water changes, cleaning and equipment upkeep can take more time than you expected. We’re building Selenoa to make that care easier, so you have more time for the part you love.

Easier water changes.

Our first focus is taking more of the work out of water changes: preparing replacement water, removing old water and managing routine exchanges. A system designed around making an essential part of aquarium care easier to keep up with.

Cartridges that simplify care.

Accessible, replaceable cartridges are central to our approach. We’re designing filtration and treatment modules to make routine replacement and servicing simpler.

Start with what you need.

Begin with the modules that help your aquarium most. Each is intended to be useful on its own, with compatible additions working together as your needs grow.

Looking further ahead. We’re researching ways to treat and reuse aquarium water, or selectively remove unwanted substances, with the aim of reducing how much water needs replacing. These approaches remain in research; any reduction in water changes will need to be demonstrated for the aquarium and treatment involved.

Intelligence, over time

Care that learns. Less for you to manage.

Selenoa’s vision is a system that cares for your aquarium, its inhabitants and its own equipment—with less need for constant supervision. It is designed to learn your aquarium’s unique responses and coordinate more of the routine locally, so you can spend more time enjoying what lives in it.

More care, handled for you.

The aim is to handle routine care, monitor the system’s own condition and make carefully bounded adjustments where they have been validated. Bring the owner in when judgment or hands-on attention is genuinely needed, with a clear explanation and next step.

Less supervision does not mean no care: cleaning, replenishment, worn parts and uncertain conditions can still need attention. Self-management and protective responses must be tested before use.

Feeding, understood in context.

Compare conditions before and after feeding, then look for recurring responses across many feeds. The aim is to recognize when a feeding routine may be adding unnecessary strain, and work toward gradual, carefully bounded adjustments.

Changes in water conditions are clues, not a measure of food eaten. Enough nutrition matters as much as water quality; automatic adjustment needs feeding safeguards and separate validation.

Care for the equipment, too.

Follow the condition of the equipment caring for your aquarium—not just whether it is switched on. Available operating measurements and service history could help identify developing restrictions, wear or performance changes and bring useful checks forward.

The goal is earlier, clearer maintenance guidance and help narrowing possible causes. Not every failure is predictable, and diagnostic claims need equipment-specific testing.

Ecosystem

Your aquariums.
One intelligent ecosystem.

An adaptable vision for freshwater, planted, saltwater and reef setups.

Start with one. Build at your own pace.

Each module is designed to manage its own core operation and monitor its own condition. Connect more modules for richer shared insight and coordination.

Independent does not mean without power or required sensors and accessories. Each module’s needs, compatible combinations and safeguards will be specified and tested.

08

The aquarium you keep

Different worlds. The same thoughtful care.

Freshwater, saltwater, planted or reef—one familiar experience, shaped around the life within.

Freshwater

Everyday care, made clearer.

Bring water conditions, filtration, temperature and feeding into one understandable picture. The aim is a simpler daily routine, with useful attention when something needs a closer look.

Saltwater

A connected view of marine care.

Bring marine-compatible sensing, circulation, temperature and water-level care together. See the available information in context, with equipment and routines suited to the aquarium you keep.

Planted

Thoughtful care for a living landscape.

Connect lighting routines, water conditions and suitable nutrient dosing with the wider health picture. Make ongoing care easier to organize around your plants and the animals that share their habitat.

Reef

Complex care. A clearer experience.

Bring lighting, water movement, temperature, dosing and supported chemistry tests into one place. The vision is less fragmented reef keeping, with useful next steps drawn from the information available.

An adaptable product vision, not a universal compatibility claim. These worlds can overlap; sensors, equipment and care routines will be specified and tested for each supported setup.
